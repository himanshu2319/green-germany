What makes Germany green?

Sustainability in Germany can be seen in waste separation, reusable products, public transportation, cycling and renewable energy. These systems are supported by everyday habits and environmental awareness.

03 • DAILY LIFE

Small habits, big impact.

Sustainability is not only about big projects. It also happens through everyday choices.

☀️

Renewable Energy

Solar and wind power play an important role in Germany's energy transition.

🫙

Reuse

Reusable bottles, containers and shopping bags can reduce unnecessary waste.

👕

Second Hand

Flea markets and second-hand shopping give products a longer life.

🔧

Repair

Repairing useful products can be better than immediately replacing them.

🥕

Local Food

Farmers' markets and local products can support nearby producers.

💡

Save Energy

Switching off unused lights and devices is a simple everyday habit.
